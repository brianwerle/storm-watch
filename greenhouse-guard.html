Real NASA story & how the shielding works

The real storm: NASA DONKI lists an X8.2 flare peaking at 16:06 UTC on September 10, 2017, linked to a GOES13 particle event at 16:25 UTC—19 minutes later. These are detector times, not a lunar arrival forecast.

Water and other hydrogen-rich materials can reduce exposure to solar particles. This game uses water-filled shielding blocks, rather than ordinary heat insulation. The animated plant responses, shield strength, and harvest are fictional game mechanics; DONKI does not supply crop damage or shielding performance data.

The blue effects show protection improving, not all radiation disappearing. Plants still need growing lights, water, air, and care. The game’s particle animation is not to scale or a radiation transport model.

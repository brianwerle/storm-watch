Moon ice, NASA, and game science

This imagined Moon base stores harvested ice below ground, melts it, and routes the water to an underground tank for later treatment and use. That is a game scenario, not a description of an operating lunar base. Recovered water supports life support.

Dark rock cannot be dug; red fissures lose water. The drops follow simplified downward/sideways routes and split at junctions. Lunar gravity, ice extraction, melting energy, water pressure, and contamination are not physically simulated. The protected channels represent a sealed water-transfer system—not pouring drinking water into exposed lunar soil.
